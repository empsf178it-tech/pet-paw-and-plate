CORE FEEDING PRINCIPLES

The Four Pillars of Wholesome Bowls

High Bioavailability

Human-grade muscle meats and organ proteins offer up to 94% amino acid absorbability compared to rendered meals.

Moisture & Hydration

Fresh meals naturally contain 65–75% cellular moisture, vital for feline urinary tract health and renal longevity.

Portion Control

Precision gram-based caloric intake prevents canine obesity while maintaining lean muscle mass.

Life-Stage Tweaks

Puppy growth requires higher calcium-phosphorus ratios, while senior pets thrive on joint-supporting Omega-3s.
